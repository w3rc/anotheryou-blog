The grievance letter for a cut or rejected claim

Send it to the insurer's grievance officer in writing, quoting the clause they used and the rule that answers it. Keep the acknowledgement.

To: The Grievance Officer, [insurer name] Subject: Grievance: claim [claim number], policy [policy number] Dear Sir or Madam, [Patient's name] was admitted to [hospital] from [date] to [date]. Claim [number] was [rejected / paid ₹X of ₹Y] by your letter dated [date], citing [the reason or clause in their letter]. [Pick the paragraph that fits, or write your own:] PRE-EXISTING DISEASE: The policy has been continuously in force since [date], including renewals and porting, which is over 60 months. Under IRDAI's rules, after 60 months of continuous cover a claim can't be rejected for non-disclosure, a pre-existing disease included, except for proven fraud. Policy schedules for each year are attached. DISCHARGE DELAY CHARGES: The hospital requested final authorisation at [time] and it was given at [time]. Under IRDAI's master circular on health insurance (May 2024) it is due within three hours, and any extra hospital charge for the delay is the insurer's to bear. DEDUCTION WITHOUT A CLAUSE: Please state the exact policy clause for each deduction, item by item. "As per policy terms" doesn't identify one. I ask you to reconsider and settle the full admissible amount of ₹[amount]. If this isn't resolved within 30 days, I will take it to Bima Bharosa and the Insurance Ombudsman. Attached: [policy schedules, discharge summary, final bill, your letter] [your name], [relationship to patient], [phone], [date]

The route, in order

#WhereWhat to knowDate done
1Insurer's grievance officerThe letter on page 1. A reply is due in about 14 days.
2Bima Bharosa, bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in, toll free 155255IRDAI's own portal. It forwards the complaint to the insurer and tracks it.
3Insurance Ombudsman, cioins.co.inOpens once the insurer rejects your grievance or doesn't reply in 30 days. File within one year of the insurer's final reply. Free, no lawyer, claims up to ₹50 lakh.
4Consumer commission, e-DaakhilEspecially above ₹50 lakh.

At the discharge desk

  • Note the time the hospital sends the final bill to the insurer. Authorisation is due within 3 hours of that request.
  • Cashless? The insurer or TPA collects papers from the hospital. You shouldn't be sent running for them.
  • Any deduction: ask for the exact clause, in writing.

One folder, starting today

  • Policy schedule for every year (proves continuous cover)
  • Claim forms
  • Discharge summary
  • Final bill with its item list
  • The insurer's letter

Usual reasons for a cut, and the question to ask

  • Room rent cap: a costlier room than the cap can shrink the whole bill in proportion. Ask which cap and which clause.
  • Sub-limit or co-payment: ask for the clause and the figure.
  • "Not covered" items: ask for the list the policy names.

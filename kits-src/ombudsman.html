Step 1: the complaint your bank has to answer

RBI won't take your complaint unless the bank heard it first, in writing. Fill in the brackets and send it through the bank's grievance page or email, or hand it in at the branch and get a stamped copy.

To: The Grievance Redressal Officer, [bank, NBFC or wallet name] Subject: Complaint about [failed transaction / card closure / loan documents / credit report / charges], [account, card or loan, last 4 digits] Dear Sir or Madam, On [date], [what happened, with amounts and reference numbers]. [If you raised it before: I raised this on (date), reference (number), and have had no resolution.] Under RBI's rules, [the rule, e.g. a failed UPI transfer must be reversed by T+1 day, with ₹100 for each day of delay after that, paid without my asking]. Please: 1. [the fix, e.g. reverse ₹X / close my card / return my original documents / correct my credit report]. 2. Pay the compensation the rule sets for each day of delay, if it applies. 3. Reply in writing with a complaint number. If I don't receive a satisfactory reply within 30 days, I will file with the RBI Ombudsman under the Reserve Bank Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2026. [your name], [phone], [email], [date]

Your dates

WhatYour date

Example: complaint received 1 December 2026, no reply. File from 31 December 2026, last day 31 March 2027.

Step 2: the Ombudsman complaint, ready to file

File at cms.rbi.org.in (Hindi or English form, your story in any language), or email crpc@rbi.org.in, or post it signed to CRPC, Reserve Bank of India, Central Vista, Sector 17, Chandigarh 160017. Free. Helpline 14448 can't take complaints.

  • Your name, mobile, email and full postal address
  • The bank's name, branch and address
  • Your complaint number to the bank, its date, and a copy of the complaint
  • Account, card, loan or transaction details, with dates and reference numbers
  • The bank's reply, if there was one
  • What happened, what it cost you, and exactly what you want done (the fix, the per day compensation, and compensation for your time and harassment)
  • Your documents: statements, screenshots, letters

5 rules where the bank pays you for every day it's late

WhatDeadlineThen the bank pays you

A card you never asked for and were billed for: the charges are reversed and you get twice the charges as a penalty.

It won't take

  • A complaint the bank never received from you first, or one filed before the 30 days or after the 90.
  • The same issue already in a court or consumer commission (a police case is fine).
  • The bank's business decisions, like refusing a loan. Housing finance companies and unregistered loan apps aren't covered.

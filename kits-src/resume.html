The one page layout, ready to fill in

One column, plain headings, contact details as text. Under 10 years of experience: keep it to this one page. Fresher? Move Education above Experience.

[Your Name]

[City] · [phone] · [email] · [LinkedIn or portfolio link]

Summary

[1 or 2 sentences: who you are, your strongest result, and the role you want. Match the job's qualifications.]

Experience

[Job title], [Employer][Mon YYYY to Mon YYYY]
  • [Accomplished X, as measured by Y, by doing Z]
  • [Accomplished X, as measured by Y, by doing Z]
  • [Accomplished X, as measured by Y, by doing Z]
[Job title], [Employer][Mon YYYY to Mon YYYY]
  • [result line]
  • [result line]
[Older role], [Employer][YYYY to YYYY]
  • [one line]
[If you choose to show a gap: YYYY to YYYY: Time off spent with family (and any course you did)]

Skills

[the tools and skills the job post names, that you really have]

Education and certifications

[Degree, College][YYYY]
[Certification, issuer, year]

Leave off: photo, date of birth, marital status, religion, father's name, full address, CTC, career objective, references, "I hereby declare".

Turn duties into results

Google's formula: Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y] by doing [Z]. X is what changed, Y is the number that proves it, Z is what you did. No exact number? Use scope: team size, customers, budget, tickets, how often. Never invent one.

Your old line (a duty)Rewritten as a result (X, Y, Z)
e.g. Responsible for social mediaGrew Instagram followers from 4,000 to 22,000 in 9 months by posting 4 reels a week

12 checks before you send

  • Saved as a PDF, named Firstname_Lastname_Resume.pdf
  • One page if under 10 years of relevant experience; never more than two
  • Freshers: education first. Working: experience first, education at the end
  • Every recent bullet shows a result, not a duty
  • Each job has employer, title and dates
  • Summary is 2 sentences at most, or left out
  • No objective, no references section, no home address
  • The job's minimum and preferred qualifications show up in your lines
  • Skills match words in the job post (only ones you really have)
  • Contact details are plain text, not icons or an image
  • Paste test: select all in the PDF, paste into a notes app, and it reads in the right order
  • A gap, if you show it, is one honest line, and you're ready to talk about it

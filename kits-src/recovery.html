The complaint letter your bank has to answer

Fill in the brackets, attach your call log (page 2) and screenshots, and send it to the bank's grievance redressal officer, named on the bank's website. In writing, never only by phone.

To: The Grievance Redressal Officer, [bank or NBFC name] Subject: Complaint against recovery agents, loan account [account number] Dear Sir or Madam, I hold loan account [number] with you. Since [date], people collecting this loan on your behalf[, from agency name, if known] have done the following: [date, time, number]: [what happened, e.g. 14 calls before 11 am] [date, time, number]: [e.g. called my brother and told him I am a fraud] [date, time, number]: [e.g. threatened arrest] RBI's rules for banks and NBFCs say a lender and its agents must not harass or intimidate, humiliate a borrower or intrude on the privacy of family members, referees and friends, make threatening or anonymous calls, call repeatedly or before 8:00 am or after 7:00 pm, or make false or misleading statements. Under these rules you are responsible for what your recovery agents do. Please: 1. Tell me the name and contact details of the recovery agency on my account. 2. Withdraw the agency from my account while this complaint is pending. 3. Reply in writing with a complaint number and the action taken. My call log and screenshots are attached. If I don't receive a satisfactory reply within 30 days, I will file with the RBI Ombudsman. [your name], [phone], [date]

After you send it

  1. Keep the complaint number. Wait up to 30 days.
  2. Rejected, or no reply in 30 days? File with the RBI Ombudsman at cms.rbi.org.in within 90 days of the bank's last reply or of the 30 day wait ending, whichever is later. Free, no lawyer, and on top of money actually lost it can award up to ₹3 lakh for loss of time, expenses, harassment and mental anguish.
  3. Threats of violence, extortion or morphed photos are crimes: police, and for anything online, 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in. A loan app with no bank or NBFC behind it isn't RBI regulated: report it on cybercrime.gov.in and sachet.rbi.org.in.

If an agent comes to your door

  • Ask for the bank's authorisation letter and an identity card. They have to carry both.
  • Note the agency name. Banks have to list their recovery agencies on their website.

3 reminder emails, from polite to the one that gets paid

Email, not calls. Polite, dated and boring wins. Send them in order, a week apart, and attach the invoice every time.

Reminder 1: a new date

Subject: Invoice [number] due [date], ₹[amount] Hi [name], Invoice [number] for [work] was due on [date] and is still open. I've attached it again. Could you confirm payment by [date, 7 days from today]? Thanks, [your name]

Reminder 2: the line finance teams move for

Subject: Second reminder: invoice [number], now [N] days overdue Hi [name], Following up on invoice [number], due [date]. I'm registered as a micro enterprise under Udyam ([Udyam number]), so this falls under the MSMED Act, 2006. One thing your accounts team may want to know: since 1 April 2024, section 43B(h) of the Income Tax Act lets a business claim a payment to a micro or small enterprise as an expense only when it's actually paid, if it's paid after the 45 day limit. Could you release it by [date]? [As our contract allows, I'm pausing further work until it's cleared.] [your name]

Only if you registered on Udyam before agreeing the work, and the client is in India. Otherwise drop the middle paragraphs.

Reminder 3: the final notice

Subject: Final notice: invoice [number], ₹[amount] Hi [name], Invoice [number] is now [N] days overdue, after reminders on [date] and [date]. Under sections 15 and 16 of the MSMED Act, 2006, payment was due within 45 days of acceptance, and late payment carries compound interest with monthly rests at three times the bank rate notified by RBI, whatever our contract says. If the full amount isn't received by [date], I'll file on the MSME Samadhaan portal for the interest as well. [your name]

Where it goes next

  • MSME Samadhaan (samadhaan.msme.gov.in). Your state's Facilitation Council tries conciliation, then decides. To challenge its award in court, the client must first deposit 75% of it (s.19).
  • Foreign client? The MSME law doesn't reach them. Use platform escrow, or advances and milestones.

3 clauses for the next contract, word for word

Advance and milestones"30% on signing, the rest in milestones as set out below."
Deemed acceptance"Deliverables are accepted unless the Client gives specific written reasons for rejection within 7 days of delivery."
Payment and a late fee"Invoices are payable within 15 days. Overdue amounts carry interest at 1.5% a month, and the Freelancer may pause work while any invoice is more than 15 days overdue."

Before the next contract

  • Register on Udyam (udyamregistration.gov.in): free, Aadhaar and PAN. The protection runs only from the registration date (Silpi Industries, Supreme Court, 2021).

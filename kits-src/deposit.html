Take this page to the stamp paper vendor

11 checks before you sign, 5 clauses to ask for word for word, and the message that gets a deposit back. Tick each box before the ink goes on.

Before you sign: 11 checks

  • Deposit amount. No national cap. Model Tenancy Act states (Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, Assam, Andhra Pradesh, some union territories) cap it at 2 months' rent. Elsewhere, ask for less or for interest on it.
  • Forfeit clause. Any line saying the "entire security deposit shall be forfeited"? Replace it with clause A below.
  • Lock-in is mutual. If you can't leave for six months, they can't ask you to leave either.
  • Painting and cleaning. No deduction "whatever the condition". Clause B limits it.
  • Return date written in. Clause C. Silence here is where months of waiting come from.
  • Registration. Over 12 months it must be registered (Registration Act, s.17). In Maharashtra every leave and licence agreement must be registered, whatever its length (s.55). A notary stamp is not registration.
  • Rent revision. Delete "may revise the rent at any time". A fixed increase at renewal (5% to 10%) is normal.
  • Inspection. At least 24 hours' written notice, except real emergencies. Clause D.
  • Repairs. Who fixes what, with a time limit for big faults. Clause E.
  • Move-in video and inventory. Every room, signed or "ok" on WhatsApp. Table on page 2.
  • Your folder. Agreement, proof of every deposit and rent payment, key handover message.

5 clauses to ask for, word for word

A. Instead of forfeiting the deposit"If the tenant leaves before the lock-in ends, the tenant will pay rent for the remaining lock-in period or one month's rent, whichever is lower, which may be adjusted from the deposit."
B. Deductions"Deductions from the security deposit are limited to unpaid rent, unpaid bills and damage beyond normal wear and tear, each supported by a bill."
C. Return date"The security deposit will be returned within 15 days of the tenant handing over the keys, with an itemised list and bills for anything deducted."
D. Inspection"The landlord may inspect the premises with at least 24 hours' written notice, at a reasonable hour, and without notice only in an emergency such as fire, flood or a gas leak."
E. Repairs"Structural, major plumbing and electrical faults are the landlord's to repair within 7 days of written notice. Minor consumables such as bulbs and tap washers are the tenant's."
Why clause A holds: under section 74 of the Indian Contract Act a court enforces reasonable compensation, not a penalty (Maula Bux v. Union of India, Supreme Court, 1969). General information, not legal advice; for a large deposit a local lawyer is worth the fee.

The deposit message, the day you hand over the keys

Hi [landlord's name], I handed over the keys to [flat address] today, [date]. Under our agreement dated [date], please return my security deposit of ₹[amount], or send an itemised list with bills for anything you deduct, by [date, 15 days from today]. The move-in and move-out videos and the inventory are attached. Bank details: [account or UPI ID]. Thank you, [your name]

Send it by email or WhatsApp, so there's a dated record.

If the date passes

  1. One reminder in writing, with a new date.
  2. A lawyer's legal notice. It costs a few thousand rupees, and many landlords pay at this stage.
  3. File: the Rent Authority or Rent Court in Model Tenancy Act states, the Small Causes Court in Mumbai, and a civil suit for recovery of money elsewhere. Consumer commissions generally don't hear deposit disputes.

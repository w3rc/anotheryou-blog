The first hour, and the letter your bank has to answer

Tick each line as you go. Then fill in the brackets and send the letter through the bank's app, email or branch, the same day. In writing, never only by phone.

  • Call 1930. Free, any time. Note the 14 digit acknowledgement number from the "XXNCRP" SMS (it starts with 3).
  • Tell the bank in writing. Ask them to block the card, UPI or net banking. Write down the date and time you reported, and the complaint number.
  • File on cybercrime.gov.in with that 14 digit number, the same day.
  • Paid by UPI? Raise a dispute inside the UPI app too.
  • Save everything: debit SMS and emails, UTR numbers, chat screenshots, the scammer's number, UPI ID, website or app.
  • Pay nobody who offers to "recover" the money for a fee. That's the second scam.

Your dates

Rule (fraud before 1 January 2027)Your date
Money left without you doing anything: tell the bank within 3 working days of its alert for zero liability
The bank credits the amount back provisionally: within 10 working days of your report
The bank decides your complaint: within 90 days of your report
No reply or a rejection? The RBI Ombudsman opens 30 days after your complaint
File at cms.rbi.org.in within 90 days of the bank's last reply or of that 30 day wait ending, whichever is later
Money on hold: after the police order, the bank pays you within 15 calendar days

Count working days Monday to Friday and skip bank holidays, so you're never a day late. If you shared an OTP or PIN, you carry the loss until you report it and the bank carries everything after, so report anyway.

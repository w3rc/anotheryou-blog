The refund ladder: 4 steps after the chatbot

The chatbot isn't the grievance officer, and a closed ticket isn't a complaint. Climb one step at a time, and write each date in.

#StepWhat it doesDate done
1The grievance officer, by email (name and email at the bottom of the site, or in the app's help or legal section)Under the E-Commerce Rules 2020 they must acknowledge in 48 hours and resolve in one month. Copy customer care. Use the email below.
2National Consumer Helpline: 1915, WhatsApp, the NCH app or consumerhelpline.gov.inFree. Forwards your docket to the company; many complaints end here. Either way it's one more dated record.
3District Consumer Commission on e-Daakhil (edaakhil.nic.in)No lawyer needed. No fee up to ₹5 lakh. File where you live or work, within 2 years. It can order the refund, compensation for harassment, and your costs.
4Bank, for "we sent it"Ask the site for the ARN or RRN and give it to your bank. That one number ends most "we sent it" arguments.

When to start climbing

Refunds to cards and UPI usually take 5 to 7 working days once a return is picked up and checked, and the bank can add a few more. Three weeks after pickup with no money is a fair point to write.

Keep these, starting now

  • Order ID and return request ID
  • Pickup confirmation: date, courier, tracking (AWB) number
  • Screenshots of the refund status, and every chat and email
  • The ARN or RRN, if the site says it has refunded

The email that gets answered (step 1)

Short, dated and calm. Consumer commissions read these later, so write it for them.

To: [grievance officer's email] Cc: [customer care email] Subject: Complaint: refund not received, order [order number] Dear Grievance Officer, I am [your name]. Order [order number], placed on [date], for ₹[amount]. [date]: return requested, request ID [ID]. [date]: picked up by [courier], AWB [number]. [date]: refund shown as [status]. No money has reached my account. [date]: support ticket [number] closed without a refund. Under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, this complaint must be resolved within one month. I am asking for a refund of ₹[amount] to my original payment method within 7 days of this email. If it is ignored, I will take this to the National Consumer Helpline and the District Consumer Commission. Attached: order confirmation, pickup confirmation, refund status screenshots, chat history. [your name] [phone]
